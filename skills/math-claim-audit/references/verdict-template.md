## Earliest unsupported step

## Adversarial test

State the smallest counterexample search, derivation check, or numerical test

## Verdict

Choose exactly one:

- proved from stated assumptions;
- conditionally valid;
- numerically supported but unproved;
- incomplete;
- contradicted;
- already known;
- unclear because definitions are missing.

## Evidence produced

List only new evidence created during this task.

## Next action

Give one bounded action. Do not propose an entire new research route.
