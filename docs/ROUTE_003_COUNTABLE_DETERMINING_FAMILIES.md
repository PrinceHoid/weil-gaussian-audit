# Route 003 — Countable determining families for Weil positivity

**Status:** Rejected as a research route; the determining-family reduction is already known and does not weaken RH.

**Scope:** Nothing in this document proves or disproves RH. It records why replacing Weil's universal quantifier by a countable determining family does not create a new route.

## Required questions

1. **Claim investigated.** Construct an explicitly enumerable countable family of admissible tests such that positivity of the Weil quadratic form on the family implies positivity on the full admissible class.
2. **Connection to RH.** Full Weil positivity is equivalent to RH. If the countable family is genuinely determining, positivity on every member is therefore another equivalent formulation of RH.
3. **Prior art.** Li's 1997 criterion already makes RH equivalent to positivity of an explicit countable sequence. Bombieri–Lagarias (1999) relate Li's coefficients to Weil positivity and the explicit formula. Bombieri (2000) studies Weil's quadratic functional as an infinite-variable quadratic form and its finite truncations.
4. **What would be new?** Countability, enumeration, density, and computability alone are not new. A genuinely new result would need a propagation theorem deriving all remaining inequalities from a strictly weaker, provable condition.
5. **First unproved step.** Prove positivity on the entire determining family. That is RH again, with a different index set.
6. **Early falsification.** Check Li 1997, Bombieri–Lagarias 1999, and Bombieri 2000 before constructing another family. They show that the proposed structural reduction is already available.
7. **Evidence level.** Literature audit plus elementary continuity argument. No numerical computation and no RH assumption.

## Why a countable family exists

Let `V` be a separable topological test-function space on which the Weil quadratic form `Q` is continuous, and let `D` be a countable dense subset. If

```text
Q(d) >= 0 for every d in D,
```

then for any `g in V`, choose `d_n -> g`. Continuity gives `Q(d_n) -> Q(g)`, hence `Q(g) >= 0`.

The convolution-square formulation does not create an extra obstacle here. If the map `g -> g * g~` is continuous, convolution squares of a dense subset are dense in the image cone: for each `g`, approximants `d_n -> g` imply `d_n * d_n~ -> g * g~`.

This is a useful logical observation but not progress on RH. The hard statement is still the infinite conjunction `Q(d) >= 0` for every determining test.

## The finite-to-infinite barrier

Finite verification of a determining family does not establish its infinite tail. Brown's 2005 paper proves a quantitative version for Li's criterion: positivity of finitely many Li-type inequalities corresponds to finite zero-free information, while the full infinite collection is needed for RH.

Bombieri's 2000 treatment likewise studies finite truncations of the infinite quadratic form and their eigenvalues. These truncations are mathematically informative, but they do not turn a finite computation into universal Weil positivity.

Accordingly, future proposals must identify a new propagation mechanism. “The family is countable,” “the family is dense,” or “each value is computable” is insufficient.

## Admissibility remains the bounded task

The repository still needs to select one exact formulation and prove that Route 001's Gaussian tests belong to it.

The handoff convention is

```text
h(r) = integral_R g(u) exp(i r u) du,
h_t(r) = exp(-(r-t)^2) + exp(-(r+t)^2).
```

In an additive Schwartz spectral formulation, `h_t` is strictly positive and Schwartz. Therefore `sqrt(h_t)` is Schwartz, and taking an inverse Fourier transform produces a Schwartz function whose Fourier modulus square is `h_t`.

This does not automatically settle a multiplicative compact-support formulation with pole-cancellation or vanishing-moment conditions. The repository must state which formulation it uses, derive the map between conventions, and verify every condition. Until then, further scaled computations are premature.

## Independent audit of Route 002

The Route 002 separator and mixing identity were independently rederived in the attached audit. This is corroboration, not human verification.

A stronger exact separator family is available. For every real `c`, define

```text
Lambda_c(f) = integral_R f(r) dr - sqrt(pi) f(c).
```

For every `c,t`,

```text
Lambda_c(h_t)
 = 2 sqrt(pi)
   - sqrt(pi) [exp(-(c-t)^2) + exp(-(c+t)^2)]
 >= 0.
```

Therefore every `f` in the closed positive cone generated by the `h_t` satisfies

```text
sup_R f <= (1/sqrt(pi)) integral_R f.
```

This excludes every nonnegative test that is more concentrated than this bound at any location, not only narrower centered Gaussians. Label this a **candidate repository-level strengthening** until independently reviewed by a human analyst.

## Recommendations for AI collaborators

1. Do not propose another countable or computable RH equivalence without first checking Li, Bombieri–Lagarias, and Bombieri.
2. Separate “determining family exists” from “positivity on the family is provable.” The latter is the entire difficulty.
3. Treat every finite verification as finite zero-free information unless a proved propagation theorem says more.
4. Attack the earliest propagation claim with counterexamples and small-eigenvalue or no-margin tests.
5. Do not start Route 004 as another reformulation.
6. Work next on the exact admissibility/convention memorandum, which is bounded and falsifiable.

## Recommendations for human collaborators

1. Have an analyst review the generalized separator `Lambda_c` and the topology of the closed cone.
2. Have an analytic number theorist select the precise Weil criterion used by the repository.
3. Add Bombieri 2000 and Brown 2005 as standing references whenever a finite determining-family computation is proposed.
4. Require any future route to contain a mechanism that is strictly stronger than enumeration, density, or finite truncation.
5. Decide whether Route 001 is a computational note or part of a broader program; do not leave that strategic choice implicit.

## Verdict

**Rejected.** The proposed countable determining-family theorem is true but structurally elementary and already subsumed by established RH criteria. It changes the indexing of the infinite burden without reducing it.

The bounded next task is not another determining family. It is a convention-and-admissibility memorandum that either places the repository's Gaussian family inside one precise Weil criterion or re-scopes Route 001 honestly.
