# Route 002 — Fixed-scale Gaussian positive-cone obstruction

**Status:** Candidate audit result; exact elementary proof, awaiting independent review.

**Scope:** This document does not prove or disprove the Riemann Hypothesis. It eliminates one proposed bridge from the restricted Gaussian family in Route 001 to Weil's universal positivity criterion.

## Proposal questions

1. **Claim.** The closed positive cone generated in the Schwartz topology by
   \[
   h_t(r)=e^{-(r-t)^2}+e^{-(r+t)^2},\qquad t\in\mathbb R,
   \]
   does not contain the narrower centered Gaussian \(G_a(r)=e^{-ar^2}\) for any \(a>1\).
2. **Connection to RH.** Even if the Route 001 functional were proved nonnegative on every \(h_t\), that result cannot be promoted to the full Weil test class merely by positive-cone density. This is an intermediate negative result about a proof route, not about RH's truth.
3. **Prior art.** Signed translates of a Gaussian have classical Wiener-type density properties because the Gaussian Fourier transform has no real zeros. Those results concern linear spans with arbitrary signs and do not transfer positivity from individual generators. The separating calculation below appears new to this repository; no claim of literature novelty is made.
4. **Novel step.** Exhibit an explicit continuous functional separating all positive mixtures of the fixed-scale family from every narrower centered Gaussian.
5. **Failure test.** Check the separator on \(h_t\) and on one \(G_a\) with \(a>1\). A sign error in either calculation would falsify the claim immediately.
6. **Dependencies.** Elementary Gaussian integration and continuity of integration and point evaluation on Schwartz space. No computation and no RH assumption.
7. **Evidence level.** Exact algebraic proof; candidate audit result until independently checked.

## Exact obstruction

Define the continuous linear functional

\[
\Lambda(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(r)\,dr-\sqrt\pi\,f(0).
\]

For every \(t\),

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}h_t(r)\,dr=2\sqrt\pi,
\qquad h_t(0)=2e^{-t^2},
\]

and hence

\[
\Lambda(h_t)=2\sqrt\pi(1-e^{-t^2})\ge0.
\]

Thus \(\Lambda\ge0\) on every finite positive combination of the \(h_t\), and by continuity on the closure of that cone.

For \(G_a(r)=e^{-ar^2}\),

\[
\Lambda(G_a)=\sqrt{\frac\pi a}-\sqrt\pi
=\sqrt\pi(a^{-1/2}-1)<0\qquad(a>1).
\]

Therefore \(G_a\) is outside the closed positive cone generated by the fixed-width \(h_t\).

## Exact one-sided scale behavior

Positive location mixing can create broader Gaussians. For \(\beta>0\), direct completion of the square gives

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\beta t^2}e^{-(r-t)^2}\,dt
=\sqrt{\frac\pi{\beta+1}}
 \exp\!\left(-\frac\beta{\beta+1}r^2\right).
\]

Consequently, after normalization and symmetrization, positive mixtures produce every centered Gaussian \(e^{-ar^2}\) with \(0<a<1\); the endpoint \(a=1\) is \(h_0/2\). The separator proves that positive fixed-scale mixing cannot produce \(a>1\). Positive location mixing broadens the Gaussian and cannot sharpen it.

## Why obvious enlargements do not complete the bridge

### Signed combinations

Classical Wiener approximation permits signed coefficients, but from \(Q(h_t)\ge0\) one cannot control

\[
Q\!\left(\sum_j c_jh_{t_j}\right)=\sum_jc_jQ(h_{t_j})
\]

when some \(c_j<0\). Approximation power and positivity preservation are different requirements.

### Derivatives in the translation parameter

Derivatives in \(t\) are limits of signed difference quotients. Restricted nonnegativity gives no sign information for \(Q(\partial_t^k h_t)\).

### Varying scale

The enlarged family

\[
h_{a,t}(r)=e^{-a(r-t)^2}+e^{-a(r+t)^2},\qquad a>0,
\]

contains the missing narrower Gaussians. But Route 001 only studies \(a=1\). It supplies no inequalities for \(Q_a(t)\) when \(a>1\), so scale variation is a genuinely new program rather than a consequence of the existing certificate.

## Admissibility caveat

The Route 001 handoff uses

\[
h(r)=\int_{\mathbb R}g(u)e^{iru}\,du,
\qquad
g_t(u)=\pi^{-1/2}e^{-u^2/4}\cos(tu).
\]

Each \(h_t\) is strictly positive and Schwartz on the real spectral line, so in the ordinary additive formulation it can be written as the squared modulus of the Fourier transform of a Schwartz function. The same is true for \(G_a\).

This does not finish the repository's stated admissibility obligation. Bombieri's multiplicative statement of Weil's criterion uses a multiplicative convolution square together with two vanishing-moment conditions. The repository has not yet derived the exact map from its additive convention to that class or verified both moments. Also, \(g_t\) changes sign for \(t\ne0\), so admissibility cannot be justified by claiming \(g_t\ge0\).
